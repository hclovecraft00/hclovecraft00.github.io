bandaid

26 October 2026 #poem

i sliced the tip of my middle finger the other day. it left a really clean cut, leaving the inner red flesh exposed. it felt as if it could split apart at the slightest tug, unravelling the fibred and reveal bone. i stuck a bandaid on it, it wasnt really going to do anything but ease my mind and censor the ugly wound. taking off the bandaid today i see the two halves conjoined once more

— harper
