escalator lovers

9 December 2025 #poem

Honey,  

i’ve grown comfortable with our journey 

a love witnessed through windowpanes

that one kopitiam,

our secondary school,

your mother’s home,

and the construction that never ends…

 

 

…I’d linger here forever

if you’d have me 

wait by the gantry 

down the long escalator 

where walls blur green to blue 

and bored witnesses

to our tender

canoooo-

-ooooood-ling 

as young lovers 

do 

i’ve arrived here once 

and again 

     and again 

and

 again

i’ll take the last train

— harper
