The average person learns more about an organisation through their social media, this is especially true for civil society groups in Singapore. An issue is that Instagram (beyond the... algo) only allows for a linear, one-way scrolling of the page. Everything is chronological, nothing is categorised. You don't really get a sense of what is important to the organisation, what they've done a lot of, very little of, or nothing of... at all!

I'm hoping to build a really lightweight and easy-to-use tool for such organisations (aka groups that need their audience to have context) to upload and categorise things easily. I begin with a familiar context -- PostGrowth Singapore and our resource hub! Basically, you send something to a telegram bot, the bot prompts you to categorise it, the bot sends it to a Google spreadsheet which then goes onto the website -- EZPZ!

We're currently still iterating... so keep a lookout
