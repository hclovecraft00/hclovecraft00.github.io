Puppet rig, or LinoxAI

A small tool for animating cut-out puppets from your own prints and drawings

28 October 2026 #article

use ithclovecraft00.github.io/puppet-rig↗

repogithub.com/hclovecraft00/puppet-rig↗

One of the great loves of my life involves the creation of lino stamps. I love how reusable and modular they are. However, the process of making stop motion is tedious (I do not have the patience to carve out 16 stamps with minute changes to limb/object positioning) and stamps are inflexible, immobile things.

Puppet Rig turns an image (a person, a scanned image of a stamp) into a jointed puppet, the way a wayang kulit puppet has a body and limbs that turn at their joints. With this tool, you can cut the pieces out of a scan, join them, then move them pose by pose like stop-motion, or live by dragging.

It can also hold several prints as one piece and switch between them frame by frame, then fill in frames between them with easing and arcs. That makes a flipbook of four lino stamps move more like a swimming fish, without carving more stamps. Sustainable (?)

Everything stays in your browser. Export as GIF or MP4.
