Summer Feeling

31 March 2026 #poem

There is little need to romanticise their absence 

Where under the scorching sun the warmth of another becomes beastly

Heaving, sticky and bothersome

When the heat incites the desire to peel off that intimate layer of skin 

Engulfing your skeletal, muscle tacked frame 

I’ll let the sun beat down on me 

Let the heat sink deep into my bones 

And the growing malaise, thick as humidity

Ooze from the skull 

Spreading across chest and limbs 

A familiar weight

An indifferent source

— harper
